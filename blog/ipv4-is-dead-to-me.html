why ipv6 is the future and ipv4 needs to die

August 1, 2026 at 06:37

Home Blog

For a while now I've been stuck in the hellhole known as IPv4 port forwarding. Yeah, its easy to do, and you can do it in seconds, but its just so annoying. I like to host MC servers for me and my friends to play on. Having to forward a different port for each one (because I occasionally have them on simultanously) is annoying. I'm migrating them to IPv6 only, and its just so much better.

The Problem:

Port forwarding is so fucking annoying. I mean it only exists because you can't give every device on (in this case) a home network a public IPv4 address. The router has to know which device wants to listen to traffic on which port, god forbid you have NAT then you have another level of translation shit. I guess it adds another layer of security, but like, thats what firewalls are for. Let your firewall do its job.

On the topic of ports, all my sites are proxied through Cloudflare. This is mainly because its fucking annoying having to open a unique port for each site. Its easier to just install their cloudflared tool and just let it map for me. I would much rather not have to worry about that stuff, and I'm going to slowly migrate my stuff away from that daemon (not off Clloudflare though, I still use them for DNS). Cloudflare is cool n all, and they have blocked a couple denial of service attacks in the pass, thank you for that. I just don't want to have to completely proxy my sites through them, when I can use them for just DNS instead.

The Solution:

I was able to obtain a /60 IPv6 block from my ISP today, and god its such a fucking blessing. My servers have their own VLAN because its just basic security, and I was able to give them the joy of their own IPv6 subnet. I can just pick my own IPv6 address in that subnet, have my MC server machine take an IP in its own /64 block, and bind the server to it. I don't have to port forward shit and connections don't have to go through NAT traversial either.

Fun Stuff:

One thing very cool about IPv6 is that you can just decide "yeah I want to have f00d in my ip" and its just like, "ok sure go ahead". It's not a super important thing to have, but its honestly cool. I'm working on giving my MC servers words in their IPs to help me differentiate at a glance without having to SSH into the machine to see what server is on what port.

Why IPv6 is Better:

IPv4 has been (at the time of writing) exhausted for a decade. IPv6 is the future. As I said before, many times, NAT traversial isn't a problem with IPv6 because IPv6 doesn't have NAT at all. Everything just works, and you can still give a device a unique public IP by just assigning it a new one on the router. One more perk, you don't even have to remember a things private IP, with direct delivery, it just routes locally regardless if you are connecting to a device on your network by its public IP.

NAT (and by extension CGNAT) is a bandaid fix to a problem IPv6 solved decades ago. Just fucking use IPv6!!! It's better than IPv4 in literally every way, and has no downsides. Everything just WORKS, no port forwarding shit either! You can host multiple things on the same port on your network and it just fucking works.

Addressing the "addresses are too long" complaint:

That argument is just so fucking stupid and wrong. You don't have to make the IPs long. If you have a block (which every sane ISP gives you), you can go as detailed or short as you want. You have quintillions of addresses to pick from. And also, IPv6 has this nifty thing where you can end an IP with ::feed:face and it fills all the remaining bits with zeros, behind the scenes, automatically. You don't have to add a bunch of random bits because there is so much room for them to just be zeros.

The Constraint (and why it's still better):

I can't remove 2600:1700:6260 from it because that's the required prefix from my ISP, but I can still give anything its own IP with that prefix. It works fine, no conflict of ports (like I have with my sites, god thats so fucking annoying). If I want to, I can just tweak on my router (or my host, depending on if the device in question uses DHCP) the IP and boom, its a new device to the global internet.

DNS makes it seamless:

99% of the time that other IP is already associated with a DNS record anyway (for example ipv6.google.com). You aren't memorizing those addresses, you just use the DNS names like you do with IPv4. In this situation DNS isn't the root of all hate and evil, IPv4 is. Want to have an easy to remember name for something? Just make a fucking DNS record like you do for IPv4. You can have BOTH IPv4 and IPv6 addresses on one record too (A & AAAA). There is literally no downside to adding IPv6 support to your records.

The Bigger Picture:

IPv4 only supports 4.3 billion addresses, and that is not nearly enough for all the fucking shit we connect to the internet. We have like 21 billion devices on the internet. Even just a /64 allocation gives you 18 quintillion addresses, far more than we as a society would ever consume. IPv4 is ancient, and IPv6 was drafted 14 years later. They could see that IPv4 could be exausted so they improved it, and solved the problem decades before it was even a thing.

I don't understand why it took so damn long for IPv6 to be implemented. If I could change history, I would've made it so IPv4 was immediately deprecated and replaced with IPv6. Its just the superior system, I literally cannot think of any real (not bullshit like "oooh its too long") excuses for not at the very least supporting it.

Why I think adoption sucks:

First off, if your ISP doesn't give you at least a /64 block of IPv6 space, your ISP is stupid and dumb. That's it. They have literally no reason to not give you IPv6 except for the fact that they don't directly get monetary benefit for doing it. Yes, its costly to implement, but god if everyone fucking did it already they would realize they have no finanical reason to renew their IPv4 lease.

Far too much shit relies on IPv4. If everyone adopted IPv6 faster, we can make IPv4 obsolete. I mean, a huge chunk of cellular networks use IPv6, and there are even some that are IPv6 ONLY. They are forced to use shitty translation mechanisms like NAT64 (welcome back the disease known as NAT) so you can connect to that shitty website that still doesn't support IPv6.

Current State:

I see no reason for my MC servers to be on IPv4 anymore. I will be migrating them all to ONLY use IPv6. I plan on migrating my websites to IPv6 as well, but this will be slower (I have quite a few to migrate). I cannot promise that my websites will continue to support IPv4 at this time, but with how fucking far behind 50% of the internet is, I guess I have to.

To all you indie game devs out there: please, I beg you, please support IPv6. Make it the default network stack in your game, let IPv4 be the fallback. It makes P2P stuff so much easier because you don't have to jump through dozens of hoops trying to punch through NAT.
